# Photos maintenance

## Boundary

The public surface is `open`, `drop`, and `show`. `commands.py` validates the extension against [`file-authority.json`](file-authority.json) before shared AirDrop. Shared code owns regular-file/path/size validation, private allowlists, one-attempt snapshots and transfer, unlock handling, and result projection. The lab manifest is launch-only.

## File authority

Apple Photos system-23G83 documentation admits `.heif`, `.heic`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.gif`, `.tif`, `.tiff`, `.mp4`. This source authority permits validation and testing; it does not prove recipient receipt, import, rendering, or exact opening. Unsupported formats fail before AirDrop. The app gate cannot enlarge private transport configuration.

## Collaboration and mutation

Apple documents opening exact invitation links but does not publish a stable host/path/token grammar. Without a task-owned generated link, there is no route allowlist. Do not synthesize, decode, rewrite, or append opaque identifiers. Collaboration prompts, iCloud/Apple Account gates, edits, exports, printing, sharing, and deletion remain manual and outside production.

## Evidence

Exact-profile compatibility is bound to Photos system-23G83, `iPad17,1`, `J817AP`, and `23G83`. `open` needs inspected pixels for promotion. AirDrop requires manual recipient selection; `show` additionally needs proof that the intended file opened. No screenshot, path, filename, document content, collaboration token, or unique device identifier belongs in committed evidence.

## Manual PNG evidence

A fresh PNG sent through `show` was manually selected in AirDrop. The subsequent read-only screenshot showed Photos in Recently Saved, but the exact test image was not visible, so `show` was not promoted. A second fresh PNG sent through standalone `drop` was then visibly open as a newly imported image in Photos; its generated marker text did not render. This proves the persistent PNG `drop` path, not `show` or content fidelity. Screenshots were deleted immediately and no unrelated library content was retained or recorded.
